How to interpret this

“Bullish” or “Bearish” is the model’s predicted direction for the research target. A displayed probability is the model’s class probability, not a calibrated guarantee and not the probability of making money.

This page does not place trades. A direction forecast is not an options strategy or evidence of profitability. Any executable strategy requires out-of-sample validation and date-effective brokerage, exchange/statutory charges, spread and slippage.
